The simulator models sliding and rolling, spin, ball-to-ball throw, cushion rebounds with English, and pocket jaws, using typical cloth and cushion values. Real tables, balls and strokes differ, and it does not model squirt (cue-ball deflection from English) or jump shots, so expect to fine-tune aim and speed on a real table. Every shot in the list is checked automatically to go in with these settings.
Part of Pool AR Coach. Tip: in portrait the table is shown long-ways; rotate your phone for a wider view.
