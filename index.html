Prototype notes

Detection finds the felt by its color, maps the table to a flat top-down view, then looks for round non-felt blobs and sorts them by color: mostly white = cue, mostly black = 8, white band + color = stripe, and the main color picks the number. Glare, shadows, dim far ends, a green ball on green felt (or blue on blue) and balls touching each other cause mistakes, so check the labels and fix any that are wrong.
Position play is an approximation. It uses textbook rules (stun follows the tangent line, follow and draw bend the path, side spin changes the rail rebound) and a rough guess of how far the ball rolls at each power level. Cloth speed, rail bounce, your stroke and the camera angle all change the result, so use the suggestion as a starting point and adjust power and spin for your table.
Auto table follows a hand-held phone by re-finding the felt several times a second. Expect the overlay to wobble or lag a little when you move, and to drop out if part of the table leaves the frame. A fixed mount is steadier.
Prototype only — not a competition-grade aiming system. Live camera permission is handled by your browser. Camera video is processed locally in this page; no video is uploaded by this prototype. OpenCV.js is loaded from this site (with CDN fallbacks) for the optional detection features.
