Placement tools

Tap a tool, then tap the camera view to add or move that marker. You can add multiple object balls and pockets. The selected object ball is the most recently placed one. For position play, also place the Next ball (N) and optionally the Next pocket (NP). Table corners takes 4 taps on the corners of the playing surface so cue-ball rail bounces line up.

Prototype notes

Automatic detection uses OpenCV.js circle detection when the library loads. Pool balls can be missed or false-detected due to reflections, shadows, and camera angle. Manually correct positions before trusting any line.
Position play is an approximation. It uses textbook rules (stun follows the tangent line, follow and draw bend the path, side spin changes the rail rebound) and a rough guess of how far the ball rolls at each power level. Cloth speed, rail bounce, your stroke and the camera angle all change the result, so use the suggestion as a starting point and adjust power and spin for your table.
Prototype only — not a competition-grade aiming system. Live camera permission is handled by your browser. Camera video is processed locally in this page; no video is uploaded by this prototype. OpenCV.js is loaded from an external CDN for the optional detection feature.
