Prototype notes

Automatic detection uses OpenCV.js circle detection when the library loads. Pool balls can be missed or false-detected due to reflections, shadows, and camera angle. Manually correct positions before trusting any line.
Prototype only — not a competition-grade aiming system. Live camera permission is handled by your browser. Camera video is processed locally in this page; no video is uploaded by this prototype. OpenCV.js is loaded from an external CDN for the optional detection feature.
